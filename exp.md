# Project Report: Multi‑Modal Wellness Platform (ZenU)

## 1. Introduction
The project focuses on developing a **multi‑modal digital wellness platform** designed to help students aged 18–22 manage stress, anxiety, and emotional challenges. By combining evidence‑based therapeutic techniques with adaptive AI personalization, the platform aims to provide **accessible, compassionate, and effective interventions** that fit seamlessly into students’ daily routines.

---

## 2. Why We Are Doing This
- **High Stress Levels in Students:** Surveys reveal that academic pressure, career uncertainty, and family expectations are the dominant stressors, with most students reporting frequent or very frequent stress episodes.  
- **Limited Use of Wellness Apps:** Over 75% of students have never used a mental wellness app, highlighting a gap in awareness and adoption.  
- **Passive Coping Mechanisms:** Current coping strategies (music, sleep, social media) provide temporary relief but lack long‑term resilience building.  
- **Demand for Personalized Tools:** Students express strong interest in features like mood tracking, reminders, and calming aids, but cite forgetfulness and privacy concerns as barriers.  

This project addresses these gaps by offering a **personalized, evidence‑based, and privacy‑focused solution** that empowers students to manage stress proactively.

---

## 3. Current Status
- **Platform Deployment:**  
  - Built using **Next.js** for cross‑platform mobile/web accessibility.  
  - **ExpressJS backend** powers AI processing and recommendation logic.  
  - **Supabase** ensures secure user data management and authentication.  

- **Implemented Modules (9 total):**  
  - Breathing techniques (Box, 4‑7‑8, Deep, Cyclic sighing)  
  - Gratitude journaling  
  - Expressive arts (mandala/scribble)  
  - Meditation (JPMR, guided imagery, grounding)  
  - AI chatbot companion (Seviyan)  
  - Mood tracking  
  - Bubble simulation (stress release)  
  - Habit visualization (Streak Garden)  

- **User Testing Insights:**  
  - High‑stress students showed meaningful reductions in stress scores.  
  - Engagement patterns revealed peak usage before sleep and during stress episodes.  
  - Students valued quick, 3–5 minute interventions integrated into their routines.  

---

## 4. Future Implementation Plans
- **Agentic AI Integration:**  
  - Develop a **recommendation algorithm inspired by X‑Algorithm and Autoresearch**, enabling dynamic selection of modules based on mood, journaling tone, engagement frequency, and personalized stress scores (PSS).  
  - Overnight iterative review loops to refine suggestions and adapt to user progress.  

- **Enhanced Creative Tools:**  
  - Expanded color palettes, brush effects, and mandala templates for deeper self‑expression.  

- **Emotion Analytics & Dashboards:**  
  - Visualize mood trends, resilience patterns, and progress insights to help users track improvements over time.  

- **Voice‑Guided Meditation & Adaptive Breathing:**  
  - Improve accessibility with audio‑guided sessions and dynamically adjusted breathing cycles.  

- **Export & Sharing Options:**  
  - Allow journaling entries and creative works to be saved or shared, reinforcing personal growth reflections.  

- **Cross‑Platform Mobile Deployment:**  
  - Extend usability across iOS and Android with 90%+ code reusability.  

- **Clinical Validation:**  
  - Conduct larger controlled trials to strengthen evidence and prepare for integration into healthcare ecosystems.  

- **Wearable Integration:**  
  - Incorporate passive biofeedback (heart rate, respiration, sleep data) to enhance precision personalization.  

---

## 5. Conclusion
This project establishes a **unique model for digital wellness** by blending artistic creativity, structured mindfulness, and adaptive AI personalization. With its emotionally aware design and evidence‑based framework, the platform is positioned to become a **humane, engaging, and truly healing solution** for student stress management. The roadmap ensures continuous innovation, moving toward an agentic ecosystem that evolves with each user’s emotional journey.
